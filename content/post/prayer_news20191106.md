<p>Some people are afraid of spiders.  I don&rsquo;t quite get it.  Some people are afraid of snakes.  OK, I can understand that one a little better.  Some people are afraid of paperwork.  That&rsquo;s me.  Paperwork sends me into a cold sweat.  I&rsquo;m just fine with spiders and snakes, though people have good reason to fear some species at least.  But paperwork?  Yep, I have actually gotten the shakes as if I were experiencing stagefright when beginning to fill out paperwork. The i130 form began the same way.</p>
<p>Since June, when Azusa was once again denied a non-immigrant visa for entry into the U.S., we have been waiting, hoping, praying, and wondering what to next.  We <em>finally</em> decided to go ahead without an attorney and seek our only known option left for Azusa:  a green card.</p>
<p>A little more than a week ago I began to fill out the petition forms that I need to send in in order for Azusa to begin the application process for a green card, the i130 and accompanying forms.  It&rsquo;s embarassing, but I did in fact experience something like performance anxiety when I began to fill them out.  However, as with most things that I know I must do, the Lord began to give me peace in my heart as I began to move forward.</p>
<p>Azusa and I have started to gather the other paperwork and evidence that we need to send in along with petition.  According to the government website, the average wait time for an answer to the petition is about ten and a half months!  Then, if accepted, Azusa will need to go through the process of actually applying for a green card, which is quite involved.  Just thinking about these things can be overwhelming and discouraging IF we allow them to be.</p>
<p>The photo of the Japanese sparrow (courtesy of Kei) at the top of the page reminded me of Jesus&rsquo; words:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Aren&rsquo;t five sparrows sold for two pennies?  Yet not one of them is forgotten before God.  In fact, even the hairs of your head are numbered.  Do not be afraid; you are more valuable than many sparrows. (Luke 12:6-7 — NET)</p>
</blockquote>

<p>Azusa and I continue to learn to trust in the One who looks upon the least of his creatures with kindness.  His plan is perfect and His ways are always right.  We can put our faith in the One who is faithful.</p>
