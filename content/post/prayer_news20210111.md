<p>In late 2000 or early 2001, while I was in Bible college, I went through an extended season of fretting and worrying about the future.  One day in class&ndash;was it &ldquo;Prison Epistles&rdquo;?&ndash;I found it particularly difficult to concentrate on the lecture.  Instead of taking notes I wrote down a simple poem aimed at dealing with fear through trusting in our faithful Savior.  Later I put it to music (though I am no musician). Now, twenty years later, we sing a version of it together as a family.</p>
<p>I hope the words will encourage you to trust in Jesus more than ever during a time in which so many people are allowing fear to be the driving force in their lives.</p>
