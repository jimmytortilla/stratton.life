<p>Personally, I (Nate) think that late September to mid-October is the most beautiful time of the year in Japan:  The HIGANBANA (pronounced &ldquo;he-gone-bonna&rdquo;), beautiful red or sometimes white flowers on long green stems, line the borders of the golden rice fields.  The air is clean and fresh; and puffy, white clouds float along in the deep blue sky.  The temperature is pleasantly warm, and, unless there is a typhoon, it is sunny almost every day.  It&rsquo;s perfect weather to take a walk and look at the fields and orchards before, during, and after the harvest.</p>

<p><strong>&ldquo;The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few.  Therefore ask the Lord of the harvest to send out workers into his harvest.&quot;</strong> ( Luke 10:2 &ndash;ESV )</p>
